Research

Security, fairness, robustness, and evaluation of AI-enabled systems.

Fairness-aware biometric security

Lightweight face PAD methods for measuring and reducing demographic disparities.

Combined Local Binary Patterns, ethnicity-aware preprocessing, group-specific threshold optimization, and statistical fairness analysis for African and East Asian populations.

Architecture and demographic bias

Comparative evaluation of convolutional and Vision Transformer architectures.

Evaluated ViT-Tiny, ResNet18, and pretrained DeiT-S across African, East Asian, and zero-shot Central Asian groups using APCER, BPCER, ACER, accuracy, and EER.

Robustness and reproducibility

Fairness evaluation under dataset shift, ablations, and random-seed variation.

Applied cross-dataset SSG and LODO protocols, five-seed replication, controlled ablations, and statistical analysis to separate stable model behavior from experimental variability.

Security research methodology

Empirical evaluation designed to survive real-world variation.

Research combines controlled experimentation, cross-dataset testing, multi-seed replication, threat-aware evaluation, and transparent reporting of uncertainty and limitations.
